1. Before you start

  • Your own email address, phone number, and identity document for OKX advanced verification.
  • A lawful source of funds and a supported way to buy or transfer USDT in your region.
  • The Roogoo deposit asset, network, address, minimum amount, and memo/tag requirement ready on the screen before you send anything.
  • A small test amount for the first transfer. Do not start with the full amount.
Important: crypto assets can lose value, and availability, fees, limits, campaigns, and identity requirements vary by jurisdiction and account. This tutorial is operational guidance, not financial or investment advice.

2. Register and verify OKX

  1. Open my OKX referral registration link.
  2. Before creating the account, confirm the referral or campaign attribution is present. OKX states that referral attribution generally cannot be added or changed after registration.
  3. Create an account with your own email or phone number, use a unique password, and complete advanced identity verification with your own information.
  4. Open Campaign Center → Welcome Rewards or the current referral page and read the exact tasks assigned to your account.
  5. Do not assume that registering alone pays 100 USDT. Rewards may require a qualifying deposit, trade, holding period, claim step, or other conditions.

3. Buy or receive USDT in OKX

Buy inside OKX

Use the payment or trading method currently available to your verified account and region. Confirm the final amount, fees, rate, and settlement time before confirming.

Receive from another platform

Use Deposit → Crypto → USDT, choose the network, and copy the address only after checking that the source platform supports the same network.

Check the minimum

The OKX deposit page shows the minimum amount and required confirmations. Sending less than the minimum may not be credited.

Keep a fee buffer

Leave enough balance for network fees and the amount Roogoo requires. Never use a guessed network or a screenshot from an old deposit page.

4. Transfer USDT to Roogoo

  1. In Roogoo, open the asset deposit page and select USDT. Copy the current deposit address.
  2. On OKX, open Assets → Withdraw → Withdraw crypto, select USDT, and paste the Roogoo address.
  3. Select the exact same network on both sides. For example, a TRC20 deposit address must be sent through the corresponding TRC20 network. The token and network must both match.
  4. Enter the amount, check the displayed fee and minimum, and complete any address, 2FA, or withdrawal verification.
  5. For the first transfer, send a small test amount. Only after Roogoo credits it should you send the remaining balance.
  6. Save the TxID and wait for the required blockchain confirmations. Do not resend because the balance is not instant.
Network mismatch can lose funds: OKX warns that unsupported crypto, wrong networks, wrong contracts, or missing tags/memos may not be credited and recovery is not guaranteed.

5. Move the balance to your Roogoo card

  1. After the OKX withdrawal is complete and Roogoo shows the funds in the asset account, open the card or asset transfer screen.
  2. Transfer only the amount needed to the Roogoo card, keeping the minimum balance and purchase-fee buffer required by that card.
  3. Check the card status, supported merchant type, expiration, authorization rules, and current fee page before adding it to Apple Account or making a purchase.
  4. Return to the Roogoo card and US App Store payment guide for Apple Account binding and purchase steps.

6. Check rewards and fix issues

How do I get the advertised reward?

The current promotion is described as up to 100 USDT, not a guaranteed fixed payout. Open the OKX Campaign Center after registration and complete only the tasks shown for your account. The amount may vary by country, campaign, account segment, and market conditions.

My deposit is not credited

Check the token, network, address, minimum amount, tag/memo, TxID, and confirmation count. If all details match, submit the transaction through OKX or Roogoo support. Do not send a second transfer to “speed it up.”

The referral was not attached

Referral attribution normally must be established at registration. If you registered without the referral link, do not create duplicate accounts to chase a reward; contact the platform support channel and follow its account rules.

7. Security checklist

  • Use only official apps or websites, enable an authenticator and anti-phishing code, and never share passwords or verification codes.
  • Check the full address and network on the confirmation screen, not only the first and last characters.
  • Never use another person's identity, bank card, account, or billing information.
  • Keep the TxID, screenshots, and support ticket number private unless official support requests them through a verified channel.
