1. What you need

  • Your own email address, phone number, and identity documents required by Roogoo's verification flow.
  • A secure password and an authenticator app. Never give verification codes or authenticator recovery keys to another person.
  • A lawful source of USDT if you plan to fund the card. Confirm the exact network before every on-chain transfer.
  • A US-region Apple Account for the US App Store, plus the real billing name and address shown by the card issuer.
  • Enough balance for the app price, possible sales tax, card fees, and temporary authorization amounts.
Important: Roogoo cards are prepaid cards, not credit cards. There is no overdraft. Digital assets, card services, reward programs, and fee schedules carry risk and can change.

3. Choose and apply for a card

First-card promotion

Current Roogoo card pages list a USD 0 issuance price for an eligible new user's first card. Confirm the price displayed immediately before applying.

7-day 0-fee trial

The current first-card trial lasts 7 × 24 hours from activation and waives listed transaction and cross-border fees for cumulative spending below USD 500. Refund, decline, reversal, dispute, and other fees are excluded.

Lifetime 0-fee upgrade

Roogoo currently describes this as a ROOG staking benefit with separate staking, lockup, yield, and redemption rules. Read the latest terms before committing funds.

App Store compatibility

Card types differ. Select only a card whose current details explicitly support App Store payments and whose issuing region fits your intended use. Compatibility is not guaranteed for every account or transaction.

Do not choose a card only by color or name. Compare issuing region, network, 3DS/AVS support, authorization fees, declined-transaction fees, minimum balance, refund rules, and supported merchants on the live card-detail page.

4. Deposit and fund the card

  1. In Roogoo, open Assets → Deposit and select the asset, such as USDT.
  2. For an on-chain deposit, select a network and use exactly the same network on the sending exchange or wallet. A network mismatch can permanently lose funds.
  3. Wait for the deposit to be credited, then transfer the required amount from the asset account to the card according to Roogoo's current card flow.
  4. Keep a buffer above the purchase amount. Roogoo currently recommends roughly 3%–5% extra for authorization and fees.
  5. Check the card is active and review its current minimum-balance and freeze rules before attempting payment.
Moving funds from OKX? Follow the OKX registration and Roogoo deposit guide for the referral link, identity verification, USDT purchase, network matching, and transfer checklist.
Avoid repeated declines: insufficient balance or rapid repeated attempts can trigger decline fees, card freezing, or merchant risk controls. Fix the cause before retrying.

5. Add the card to a US Apple Account

  1. Confirm the Apple Account country or region is United States. Apple requires the payment method to be supported in the account's region.
  2. Open the Roogoo card details and note the cardholder name, number, expiration date, security code, and billing address supplied for that card.
  3. On iPhone or iPad, open Settings → your name → Payment & Shipping → Add Payment Method.
  4. Enter the card information and the billing name and address exactly as recorded by the issuer. Do not substitute a generated tax-free address.
  5. Complete any verification requested by Apple, Roogoo, or the issuing institution, then wait for the payment method to appear in the account.
Apple Pay is different: adding a card to Apple Wallet does not automatically add it as the Apple Account payment method. App Store billing is managed under Payment & Shipping.

Apple lists most credit and debit cards as accepted in the United States, but acceptance does not guarantee that every prepaid card, issuer, account, or transaction will pass merchant and issuer checks.

6. Purchase an app

  1. Check the final App Store price and possible sales tax, then ensure the Roogoo card has enough balance plus a reasonable fee and authorization buffer.
  2. Open the paid app in the US App Store, tap the price, and confirm with Face ID, Touch ID, or the Apple Account password.
  3. Check both Apple purchase history and the Roogoo card transaction record after payment.
  4. For subscriptions, leave enough balance before every renewal or cancel before the renewal date. A failed recurring payment can create account or card issues.
  5. If Apple declines the payment, stop repeated attempts and work through the checklist below first.

7. Troubleshooting and security

The card cannot be added or is declined

  • Check that the Apple Account region, card issuing region, billing name, billing address, and ZIP code match the issuer's records.
  • Check available balance, card status, spending limits, supported merchant categories, and whether the selected card supports App Store payments.
  • Look for pending authorization or 3DS verification, then contact Roogoo support or Apple Support with the exact error. Never send a full card number, password, or verification code.

The 1U reward has not arrived

Confirm that vtqst0 was attached before registration was submitted, then check the current promotion's region, identity-verification, card-activation, spending, time-limit, and new-user requirements. Rewards are controlled by Roogoo; use its support channel if all listed conditions are complete.

Security checklist

  • Use only the official Roogoo domain, enable an authenticator, and use a unique password.
  • Test with a small amount first and verify the deposit network at both ends.
  • Never rent, lend, or sell an account or card, and never use another person's identity or billing information.
  • Review fees before every card application, transfer, purchase, refund, cancellation, or dispute.
