Timeline editing

Stitch multiple clips, trim, split, reverse, rotate, flip, add borders, and adjust playback speed.

Captions and overlays

Add captions, stickers, cover titles, doodles, mosaic areas, and picture-in-picture frames.

Free and paid editions

The free edition can show Yandex Mobile Ads through Yandex Mobile Mediation, with Google AdMob configured as a demand source in the Yandex dashboard. App open, editor banner, native/feed, and post-export interstitial placements are enabled only in the free build after ATT status is resolved. The paid build is ad-free and does not initialize advertising SDKs.

Screenshots

Current screenshots show timeline editing, automatic captions, and export workflows.

Local-first Workflow

ArtCut does not require an account. Imported media, drafts, generated renders, extracted audio, and caches are stored on device by default. Users control export and sharing.
