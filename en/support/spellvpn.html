Editions and version information

SpellVPN Tool · iOS / Mac

SpellVPN Tool is a paid, ad-free configuration client for iOS and macOS. It supplies no server nodes, preset source catalog, or node-service subscription. Import your own configuration, nodes, or subscription URL that you are authorized to use. The client can establish a VPN connection using the imported configuration; it is not an offline-only file viewer.

SpellVPN Ultra · iOS

SpellVPN Ultra is the iOS edition with advertising and optional App Store auto-renewing subscriptions. A valid subscription removes ads and gives access to Curated Sources and Exclusive Lines supplied through the service. Ultra also supports users' own imports. These service-provided configurations are an actual subscription benefit, not just ad removal.

Configuration sources and network requests

Configuration sources and network requests

SpellVPN Tool contacts the subscription, DNS, and server endpoints in your imported configurations. SpellVPN Ultra also requests Curated Sources and Exclusive Lines from apixx.spellv.com when using those features, and may then contact the returned subscription and node endpoints. Remote services receive the network information needed to handle these requests, such as IP address and HTTP request metadata. VPN traffic is processed by the servers selected in the active configuration. This is separate from App Store purchase processing and Ultra advertising.

Ultra subscriptions and access

In SpellVPN Ultra, open Settings → Subscription → Ultra Subscription to view the available plans, purchase, or restore a purchase. With an active entitlement, use Curated Sources or Exclusive Lines in the normal interface. My Imports remains separate from service-provided content. If a purchase or source cannot be loaded, contact support with the app version and error text; do not send passwords or complete subscription URLs.

Ultra subscriptions and access

Weekly, monthly, quarterly, and yearly auto-renewing plans are handled by Apple. The App Store purchase sheet shows the actual price, period, and renewal terms. You can restore eligible purchases in the app and manage subscriptions in your Apple account. Source contents and connectivity can change; access is not a promise of permanent nodes, unrestricted traffic, or a particular region's availability.

Common Questions

Why does connection fail?

Check that the imported configuration is valid, the server is reachable, and the required system permission was approved. On iOS, approve the VPN permission prompt. On macOS, the signed app and Packet Tunnel extension must have the correct Network Extension entitlement and provisioning profile.

Which SpellVPN edition has ads or subscriptions?

Tool: paid, ad-free, user-supplied configurations only. Ultra on iOS: advertising and optional subscriptions that remove ads and provide Curated Sources and Exclusive Lines.

How can I import a configuration?

Use QR scan, photo QR, clipboard, Files, Wi-Fi Upload, subscription URL, proxy link, system share, or the SpellVPN URL schemes. Supported direct links include ss, vmess, vless, trojan, hysteria2, hy2, tuic, socks, socks5, HTTP, and HTTPS proxy URIs.

Does SpellVPN for macOS include ads or subscriptions?

No. The paid macOS target has no ad SDKs, no analytics SDKs, no StoreKit products, no subscriptions, and no built-in VPN provider account. It shares the paid App Store listing with the iOS paid edition, but the Mac build itself is separate from the iOS Ultra advertising and subscription build.

What can the Mac version import and manage?

The Mac version can import sing-box JSON, Clash/Mihomo YAML, common proxy links, SIP008 Shadowsocks JSON, WireGuard text, QR images, clipboard text, files, and remote URLs. It can manage profiles, node groups, favorites, source updates, route rules, local privacy filters, Relay / MultiHop, diagnostics, encrypted backups, and menu bar connection controls.

What is included in backups?

Full app data export and iCloud Drive backup can include configs, nodes, subscriptions, policy groups, rules, DNS, route mode, virtual adapter settings, and traffic stats. Logs and installed iOS VPN permission are not part of the normal backup.

Can SpellVPN provide a server or subscription?

Tool: paid, ad-free, user-supplied configurations only. Ultra on iOS: advertising and optional subscriptions that remove ads and provide Curated Sources and Exclusive Lines.

Where are my configs stored?

Configs and logs are stored on your device unless you export, share, or back them up through iCloud Drive. Clearing local data does not delete iCloud Drive backup files.
