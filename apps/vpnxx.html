Free and Pro

VPN XX Free can show Yandex Mobile Ads through Yandex Smart Waterfall, with Google AdMob configured as a mediated demand source. The free build can use bottom banner, app-open, interstitial, and native in-feed placements around list browsing, import, refresh, copy, share, and speed-test workflows. VPN XX Pro is a paid download with no ads and does not initialize advertising SDKs.
