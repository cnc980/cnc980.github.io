Release history and developer statement

ArtCut and Pro share core video-editing technology. The free edition includes advertising. Local replacement work addressed permission/consent ordering and startup/export reliability; upload and release of those fixes have not been confirmed. The app is expressly included even if it could not be selected among the form's five entries.

The compile-time AppVariant excerpt selects the free edition and advertising through ARTCUT_FREE. The tracking manager excerpt shows the inspected permission path. Neither excerpt proves that a particular replacement build was uploaded or approved.

Next steps / not yet verified as complete

If Apple requires a unified listing, we are willing to consolidate the editions. We ask whether any specific ArtCut conduct contributed to the account action rather than assuming it was only a VPN-app issue.
