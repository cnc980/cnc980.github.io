Seven listings, not five

The developer reports that the appeal form allows only five app entries. That interface limit must not narrow this account-level appeal. The seven confirmed listings are included below; iOS and Mac entries may be listed separately in the form. The two preserved screenshots show per-app fields and a character limit, not the five-entry cap. This is not presented as an independently verified Apple-wide rule.

EasyTallys appears first because it has a documented prior issue. This ordering does not establish which app caused the account decision.

Supplement to the pending account appeal

We request reconsideration of the pending membership termination and an opportunity to have corrected submissions reviewed. We acknowledge the specific issues and disclosure gaps described app by app below. We ask App Review to assess the entire account, not only the entries selectable in the form, and to identify any additional app, platform, build, feature, content source, or communication relied upon. If conduct involving another listing contributed to the decision, please specify it so we can respond with relevant facts. This is a supplement to the appeal already reported as queued, not a claim of approval or a request to bypass review.

Status is historical, not live: the developer supplied the September 2 notice and later correspondence confirming the appeal was queued. No restored membership, app approval, or termination hold has been confirmed. Updating this site does not modify installed binaries or App Store metadata.

How to read this evidence

The record combines developer-confirmed release history, current local source excerpts, local metadata drafts, and two original developer-supplied form screenshots. These are different evidence types. No exact historical upload archives, Apple upload receipts, production service authorization documents, or new App Store approvals are included. A SHA-256 digest identifies the captured bytes; it does not certify that Apple reviewed or received them. Selected excerpts are not an exhaustive code or binary audit.

This is a public, sanitized record with identical content for all readers. It omits personal account identifiers, private correspondence, node credentials and working public subscription URLs. It is not password protected. Original materials can be provided privately to Apple through an appropriate channel if requested. Search-engine noindex is a preference, not access control.

Evidence register

Each downloadable item states its provenance, limitations and hashes. The full record contains the bilingual app statements and all text excerpts; the original PNG files are available separately below.
